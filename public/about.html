About us

About Palm Horizon

A twelve-room seaside resort where every booking is read and confirmed by a person at the front desk.

Who we are

Palm Horizon Resort & Spa is a small resort with twelve rooms over three floors. Ten different room styles mean there's a fit for most trips: a single traveller on a budget, a couple celebrating something, or a family who needs space for four.

Because the resort is small, we don't hand out rooms automatically. When you send a booking request, someone at the front desk reads it, checks which rooms are free, and assigns the one that suits your request best. You'll see that happen in your account as your booking moves from Pending to Confirmed.

Our rooms, floor by floor

Every room number and room style at the resort. The standard rooms are on the first floor and the suites are on the top floor.

How we look after you

A person checks every booking

Online requests are reviewed by the front desk, who assign your room by hand and confirm it in your account.

Prices you can check

You pay the nightly rate times the number of nights, plus 5% tax. The total is shown before you book and on your reservation.

Your booking, your account

Only you and our front desk can see your reservations. You can cancel a request yourself while it's still pending.
