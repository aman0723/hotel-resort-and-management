Book direct

Why book direct

Booking on this website keeps everything in one place: your request, its status, your room and your total.

Instant reference

The moment you send a request you get a booking reference, like RES-4F2A9C. It's saved to your account, so you never need to hunt for an email.

Track every stay

My reservations shows each booking's dates, room, total and status. When the front desk assigns your room, the room number appears there.

Cancel freely

While a request is still pending you can cancel it yourself with one click. No phone call, no form.

How booking works

  1. Choose a room and dates

    Pick a room style, your check-in and check-out dates and the number of guests. Each room shows how many people it sleeps.

  2. Sign in or create an account

    An account keeps your bookings together. It takes your name, email and a password, nothing else.

  3. Send your request

    Your booking is saved straight away with a reference and the status Pending. You'll see the full price before you send it.

  4. The front desk confirms it

    Staff assign a specific room of the style you chose. Your booking changes to Confirmed and shows your room number.

  5. Arrive and check in

    Check-in is from on your arrival date and check-out is by on your departure date.

How your price is worked out

Your total is the room's nightly rate times the number of nights, plus 5% tax. There are no booking fees. The same total is shown on the booking page, on your reservation and to the front desk.

Changed your mind about a confirmed booking? Contact us with your booking reference and we'll sort it out.
