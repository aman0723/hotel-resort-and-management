Frequently asked questions

How do I book a room?

Choose a room on the Rooms & Suites page or use the booking bar on the home page. You'll need to sign in or create an account, then send your request. See how booking works.

When is my booking confirmed?

Your request is saved as Pending as soon as you send it. It becomes Confirmed when the front desk assigns you a room, and the room number then appears on My reservations.

Can I cancel?

Yes. While a booking is Pending you can cancel it yourself from My reservations. For a Confirmed booking, send us a message with your booking reference.

How is the price calculated?

The nightly rate times the number of nights, plus 5% tax. The exact total is shown before you book.

How many guests can stay in a room?

It depends on the room, from one guest in the Standard Room up to five in the Presidential Suite. Each room's limit is shown on the Amenities page and when you book.

Do I need an account?

To book, yes. Your account keeps your reservations together and lets you track and cancel them. You can browse rooms and send us a message without one.
