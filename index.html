How booking works

Choose a room style and your dates, and your request is saved to your account straight away with a reference code. Our front desk then reviews it and assigns you a specific room, and you can watch the status change from Pending to Confirmed under My Reservations.

Rooms range from a single-bed standard room to the presidential suite with its own living area. Families can take the two-bed family room with a balcony; couples, the honeymoon suite with a jacuzzi.
