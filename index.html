Our approach

We keep the resort small on purpose. With twelve keys, the front desk knows every guest by name, and your room is assigned by a person, not an algorithm. When you book online, our team confirms the request and matches you with the right room.

Every room has air conditioning and Wi-Fi. Many have a sea, pool or garden view. Families can take a two-bed room with a balcony, couples the honeymoon suite with its jacuzzi.
